Topic Overview & Focus

Project Topic & Specific Angle

This topic mainly focuses on the economy in the Southern Colonies of Colonial America, specifically in Virginia, with its high concentration of slavery, which can also fall under the category of labor systems. Additionally, it focuses on the framework of society during the time. For instance, Virginia planters were able to rationalize enslaving African people as basic to the plantation economy and no longer felt obligated to try to Christianize enslaved people.
