Connection to Modern Society

How does this connect to Modern Society?

Moving from temporary servants to permanent, race-based slavery in colonial Virginia did more than just provide workers for tobacco fields. It created a strict social system in which skin color determined a person's legal rights. Centuries later in modern American society, this system still remains with the ideology of determining one's worth based on culture and skin color. This can be seen with racism, which is a heavily present topic in modern-day society where people are judged based on their skin color, similarly to how those who were black were labeled as enslaved and lower beings in Colonial Virginia. Although racism has been addressed with various protests and activities and has improved over time, it is still a major flaw within our society, making it evident that the very systems, specifically where blacks were considered enslaved, formed during the development of slavery and labor systems in Colonial Virginia are still connected and present in our society today.
