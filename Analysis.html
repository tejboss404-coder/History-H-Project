Historical Analysis

1. How did it shape daily life & structure in Colonial America?

Enslavement in Colonial Virginia negatively altered relationships between the English and African people, as they enslaved people to grow tobacco and other goods. This began as a result of the English being unwilling to come to Virginia as indentured servants, which led them to force Africans into enslavement.

2. What was the long-term impact on US History?

This created a significant lasting impact in American history, as following this, they made a law that further implemented slavery in the colony. Furthermore, it shaped their present-day culture, as Africans were forced to come to the US, and with them came the Akan Culture, which combined with U.S. ideals, facilitated the formation of today’s African American culture.
